# Universal SaaS POS + ERP — User Guide

## 1. Getting Started

### Logging into Your Workspace
1. Navigate to the platform URL (e.g., `https://app.posplatform.com/login`).
2. Enter your business email address and password.
3. If your email is registered across multiple businesses, you will be prompted to select your active workspace.
4. Upon successful authentication, you will be directed to your dashboard.

---

## 2. Point of Sale (POS) Checkout

### Operating the POS Terminal
1. Open **POS Terminal** from the sidebar navigation.
2. **Adding Items**:
   - Click on any product card in the catalog grid.
   - Or scan the item barcode using a hardware barcode scanner.
   - Use category tabs to filter products (e.g. Beverages, Food, Electronics).
3. **Holding & Resuming Sales**:
   - To pause an order for a customer who needs to grab another item, click **Hold Sale**.
   - To restore the order, click **Resume Sale** and select from the parked transactions list.
4. **Processing Payment**:
   - Click **Pay Now**.
   - Choose payment method: **Cash**, **Card**, or **Split Payment**.
   - For split payments, input the cash amount and card amount. The system will compute exact change due.
   - Click **Complete Sale**. A printable receipt will generate immediately.

---

## 3. Inventory & Purchasing (GRN)

### Receiving Stock via Purchase Order
1. Navigate to **Purchases (GRN)**.
2. Click **+ Receive Stock (GRN)**.
3. Select the Supplier, choose the product, input the received quantity, and enter unit cost.
4. Click **Confirm Receipt**. Your available stock balance will increment automatically, and an audit trail will be logged in inventory history.

---

## 4. Operating Expenses

1. Navigate to **Expenses**.
2. Click **+ Add Expense**.
3. Select Category (Rent, Utilities, Maintenance, Petty Cash), input the amount and date.
4. Click **Save Expense**. The expense is automatically factored into your Net Profit reports.

---

## 5. Sales Returns & Refunds

1. Navigate to **Sales & Invoices**.
2. Locate the transaction invoice.
3. Click **Issue Refund**.
4. Enter the reason for return and confirm. The sale status will update to `refunded`, and returned items will be automatically restocked into inventory.
